About VERDICT AI

Decision intelligence for better investment judgment.

Mission

To help investors think more clearly in uncertain markets.

VERDICT AI is designed to combine behavioural insight, technical context, and market intelligence so that decision-making is grounded in evidence rather than emotion.

Why it exists

Market participation is increasingly crowded with information, noise, and urgency. Investors often act from emotion, bias, or incomplete context when the cost of a mistake is high.

Our vision

To create a calmer, more disciplined investing experience powered by AI-assisted reasoning, clearer trade context, and stronger self-awareness.

Problems we aim to solve

FOMO buying, panic selling, hype-driven narratives, weak trade review, and poor understanding of how prior decisions played out in later market moves.

How AI supports analysis

AI summarises patterns, contextualises market action, identifies behavioural signals, and helps users compare multiple perspectives before committing capital.

Who benefits

Retail investors, active traders, students of markets, portfolio managers, and anyone who wants to understand the reasoning behind price moves and their own investing behaviour.

Eight core features

  • Circuit Breaker: Helps investors recognise emotional decisions such as panic selling and FOMO buying.
  • Move Autopsy: Breaks down market movements into estimated technical, fundamental, emotional and suspicious factors.
  • Stress Test: Explores hypothetical market scenarios and their possible impact on portfolios.
  • Bull vs Bear Debate: Presents opposing bullish and bearish perspectives.
  • Regret Replay: Compares previous investment decisions with subsequent market performance.
  • Hype Radar: Analyses unusual social-media activity and potential hype patterns.
  • Earnings Call Analysis: Examines management language, evasiveness and consistency across earnings calls.
  • Investor DNA: Helps users understand their investing habits and behavioural biases.
